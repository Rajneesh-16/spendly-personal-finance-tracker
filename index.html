FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Spendly free?

Yes. This frontend prototype has no paid service or backend.

Where is my data stored?

Your Spendly data is stored in your browser's localStorage.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The dashboard is responsive and adapts to phones and tablets.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings includes JSON export and import tools.

Does Spendly have real authentication?

No. Login/signup is only a frontend demonstration; it is not secure authentication.
